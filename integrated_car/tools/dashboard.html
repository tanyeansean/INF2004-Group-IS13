Calibration guide (press when the car powers on)

Not runningThe car calibrates its line sensors right after power-on; without the USB console you cannot see the prompts. This timer shows what to do when.
Sequence: ~10 s boot, 3 s warning, then hold both front sensors on the BLACK line (3 s), 3 s warning, then on WHITE (3 s). Calibration only "passes" properly with real contrast, so do it on the track.
